About Us

Learn about our story, our values, and the people behind SOHAIL KHAN MATERIAL.

Sohail Khan Materials has been a trusted and well-established name in the beads and materials business since 1995. With more than two decades of experience, we have grown into one of the biggest and most reliable suppliers in the industry. We deal in a wide range of high-quality products including Crystal Beads, Plastic Beads, Glass Beads, Charms, and much more.

Our long journey reflects our commitment to quality, honest dealing, and complete customer satisfaction. We take pride in offering the best materials at competitive prices to meet the needs of our valued customers.

Our shop is located at Shop No. 4, Bukhari Masjid, New Neham Road Off. Merriot Road, Boulton Market, Karachi. For orders and inquiries, you can contact us at 0321-5186061 (Chahat Khan) or 0304-2305076 (Karman Khan).

At Sohail Khan Materials, we continue to serve our customers with the same dedication and excellence that has defined our business for over 25 years.
