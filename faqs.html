Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and services.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3–5 business days within the contiguous United States. Express shipping (1–2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. We process all orders within 24 hours of purchase.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be in their original, unused condition with all packaging intact. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the returned item. Custom orders are non-refundable.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are securely encrypted through our payment processor.

How should I care for my beads and gemstones?

Store your beads and gemstones in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid contact with perfumes, lotions, and harsh chemicals. Clean with a soft, dry cloth. For porous stones like pearls and turquoise, avoid getting them wet.

Do you offer bulk ordering for businesses?

Yes! We offer wholesale pricing for bulk orders of 50 items or more. Please contact us at wholesale@thebeadmarket.com with your requirements, and our team will provide a custom quote within 2 business days.

Can I request a custom order?

Absolutely! We love custom projects. Whether you need a specific color combination, size, or material, we can help. Contact our team at info@thebeadmarket.com with your specifications, and we'll work with you to create the perfect product.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7–14 business days depending on the destination. Please note that customs fees or import duties may apply and are the responsibility of the recipient.

What if my item arrives damaged or is missing?

We carefully package every order, but if something arrives damaged or is missing from your order, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Send photos of the issue to support@thebeadmarket.com and we'll arrange a replacement or refund right away.
